# Backend Webhook Delivery and Signing Policy

Receivers need to trust and safely process webhooks. This policy defines how outbound webhooks are signed, delivered, and retried.

- **Owner:** Backend maintainers
- **Review cadence:** every 6 months
- **Status:** Active

## Signing

Each delivery carries a timestamp and an HMAC-SHA256 signature over the timestamp and raw body, computed with a per-endpoint secret. Secrets are generated by us, shown once, and can be rotated with an overlap period.

## Replay protection

Each event has a unique id and the signed timestamp. Receivers should reject deliveries older than 5 minutes and ignore event ids they have already processed.

## Delivery and retries

A 2xx response within 10 seconds is success. Otherwise retry with exponential backoff and jitter for up to 24 hours, then mark the delivery failed and disable endpoints that keep failing. Ordering is not guaranteed, so receivers use event ids and timestamps.

## Security and logging

Webhook targets must be HTTPS and pass the outbound allowlist and address checks in OUTBOUND_ALLOWLIST.md. Deliveries and responses are logged without secrets.

## Ownership and review

Owned by the backend maintainers. Reviewed every six months.
