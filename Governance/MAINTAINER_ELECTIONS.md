# Maintainer Elections and Removal

## Purpose

This policy defines how maintainers are elected and removed by vote. It
supersedes any inconsistent appointment or removal procedure in
`Governance/MAINTAINERS.md`.

## Nomination

Any contributor may nominate a candidate in a public issue or pull request. A
nomination includes:

- the candidate's confirmation that they are willing to serve;
- a sponsoring maintainer;
- evidence of sustained contribution, review, or stewardship in the proposed
  area of responsibility; and
- the requested areas of responsibility.

The nomination remains open for at least seven calendar days for questions and
feedback. The candidate may respond to feedback but does not vote on their own
election.

## Election vote

After the nomination period, a maintainer opens a vote that records the
candidate, proposed responsibilities, eligible voters, opening and closing
dates, and the required approval threshold. Eligible voters and quorum are
determined under the quorum policy.

Each eligible voter records a yes, no, or abstain ballot in the election record.
An election succeeds only when quorum is met and the maintainer-election
approval threshold is satisfied. The result, tally, and any dissent are
recorded with the nomination.

After a successful election, the roster is updated in a separate governance
change. The elected maintainer receives only the review, merge, and access
rights approved for their listed responsibilities.

## Removal for cause

A maintainer may be removed for cause, including a serious or repeated Code of
Conduct violation, misuse of project access or funds, material breach of
maintainer responsibilities, or sustained inactivity after notice.

A removal proposal documents the conduct or inactivity at issue and the
requested action. The affected maintainer receives notice and a reasonable
opportunity to respond, unless doing so would compromise a security,
Code of Conduct, or legal process. The affected maintainer is recused and does
not count toward eligibility, quorum, or approval for the removal vote.

The remaining eligible maintainers vote using the maintainer-removal approval
threshold. The decision record states the effective date, resulting access and
roster actions, and a non-sensitive rationale. Confidential evidence is kept
only in the applicable restricted process; the public record does not expose
personal or security-sensitive information.

## Voluntary departure

A maintainer may step down at any time by notifying the other maintainers. The
departure and resulting roster change are recorded without an election.
