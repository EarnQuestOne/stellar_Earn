# Governance Maturity Checklist

Use this checklist during a quarterly self-audit or before a material change
to governance. Score each area from 0 to 3 and keep evidence links with the
result.

| Area | 0: absent | 1: emerging | 2: defined | 3: practiced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | No accountable owner. | Owners are known informally. | Roles and backups are documented. | Ownership is reviewed and works during handoffs. |
| Decision-making | Decisions are ad hoc. | Some decisions are recorded. | Decision authority and escalation are documented. | Decisions are consistently recorded and revisited. |
| Contribution and review | No consistent review path. | Review expectations vary. | Contribution and PR rules are documented. | Review quality and turnaround are monitored. |
| Release and change safety | No shared safety process. | Safety practices exist for some changes. | Release, freeze, and hotfix paths are documented. | Drills, retrospectives, or evidence show the paths work. |
| Security and incident response | No documented response. | Contacts or procedures are incomplete. | Disclosure and response responsibilities are documented. | Response is exercised and lessons are tracked. |
| Records and transparency | Decisions are not discoverable. | Records exist in scattered locations. | Canonical records and templates are defined. | Records are current, searchable, and used. |

## Scoring

Add the six area scores for a total from 0 to 18:

- **0-5: Initial.** Establish ownership, a decision path, and basic records.
- **6-11: Developing.** Fill documented gaps and assign dates for review.
- **12-15: Defined.** Operate the documented processes and collect evidence.
- **16-18: Practiced.** Maintain the processes through measurement and periodic
  improvement.

## Audit record

Record the audit date, participants, total score, evidence, and the three most
important actions. Each action should have an owner, an issue, and a target
date. Re-score after the actions are complete rather than treating the score
as a permanent classification.
