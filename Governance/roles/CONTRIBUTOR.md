# Contributor Role

## Purpose

This document describes what it means to be a contributor to StellarEarn: what
is expected of you, what you may do, and how the role grows into the other roles
in [../ROLES.md](../ROLES.md). It complements
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), which covers the mechanics of
submitting work, and [../MENTORSHIP.md](../MENTORSHIP.md), which covers support
for new contributors.

## What a contributor is

A contributor is anyone who improves the project: code, contracts, tests,
documentation, design, translation, triage of reported bugs, or community
support. There is no membership gate and no application — opening your first
issue or pull request makes you one.

Everyone who opens an issue, comments on a proposal, or submits a pull request
is entitled to be treated with the respect required by
[../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), regardless of the size of their
contribution.

## Expectations

Contributors are asked to:

- **Follow the project policies** — the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md),
  [commit](../COMMIT_POLICY.md) and [pull request](../PR_GUIDELINES.md) policies,
  and the [security policy](../SECURITY.md).
- **Keep changes focused** — one purpose per pull request, with the issue linked,
  per [../PR_GUIDELINES.md](../PR_GUIDELINES.md).
- **Include evidence** — reproduce bugs, add tests, and document changes where
  the issue calls for it.
- **Respond to review** — engage with review comments in good faith; reviewers
  are expected to explain the "why," and contributors are expected to either
  address feedback or explain a reasoned disagreement.
- **Be patient and kind** — maintainers and reviewers are volunteers; review
  turnaround follows the targets in [../roles/REVIEWER.md](REVIEWER.md).

## Permissions

A contributor may:

- open issues and pull requests and comment on both;
- fork the repository and work in a personal branch (see
  [../FORK_POLICY.md](../FORK_POLICY.md));
- propose changes to any file, including governance documents, through a pull
  request; and
- claim unassigned `good-first-issue` issues per
  [../MENTORSHIP.md](../MENTORSHIP.md).

A contributor's approval does **not** satisfy a review requirement, and a
contributor cannot merge to the default branch; those are
[reviewer](REVIEWER.md) and [maintainer](MAINTAINER.md) authorities.

## The contribution ladder

The role is the first rung of a documented path. Each step is earned by
demonstrated work, not by time served:

1. **Contributor** — you are here: submit changes and participate in review.
2. **Reviewer** — after a sustained history of good contributions and reviews in
   an area, you may be nominated to review changes there
   ([REVIEWER.md](REVIEWER.md)).
3. **Maintainer** — reviewers who demonstrate judgement across an area, and are
   trusted to merge, may be appointed maintainers ([MAINTAINER.md](MAINTAINER.md)).
4. **Specialist roles** — triager, release manager, or security response team
   member, as described in [../ROLES.md](../ROLES.md).

There is no obligation to climb: contributing well at any rung is valuable, and
nothing here is a prerequisite for having your work accepted.

## Recognition

Contributions are credited in the change history and release notes. Contributors
who move into another role are recorded in
[../MAINTAINERS.md](../MAINTAINERS.md); moving up does not erase the fact that
the work started as a contribution.
