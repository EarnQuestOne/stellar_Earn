# Anti-Harassment and Safety Policy

This document describes StellarEarn's commitment to a safe, harassment-free
environment for all contributors, the behaviours that are prohibited, and the
support resources available to anyone who experiences or witnesses a safety
concern.

## Commitment

StellarEarn is committed to providing a welcoming and safe experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Harassment in any form — whether in code reviews, issue comments, pull
requests, community chat, video calls, or any other project space — is not
tolerated. This policy applies to all contributors, maintainers, and
participants, including those in leadership roles.

## Prohibited behaviours

The following behaviours are prohibited in all project spaces:

- **Harassment** – offensive comments, deliberate intimidation, stalking,
  sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- **Discrimination** – treating individuals differently or unfairly based on
  any protected characteristic listed in the commitment above.
- **Threats** – explicit or implied threats of violence, harm, or
  retaliation directed at any individual or group.
- **Doxxing** – publishing or threatening to publish another person's private
  information (address, phone number, workplace, etc.) without their consent.
- **Unwanted contact** – continuing to contact someone after they have asked
  you to stop, across any channel.
- **Retaliation** – taking adverse action against someone for reporting a
  safety concern, participating in an investigation, or supporting another
  person who raised a concern.
- **Abuse of power** – using a position of authority within the project to
  coerce, intimidate, or silence contributors.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive. The guiding standard is whether
behaviour makes the environment less safe or welcoming for others.

## Reporting a safety concern

Safety concerns may be reported through the same channels described in
[COC_REPORTING.md](COC_REPORTING.md):

1. **Email** – the private CoC Committee address listed in
   [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
2. **Direct message** – any CoC Committee member via the platform listed in
   [COMMUNICATION.md](COMMUNICATION.md).
3. **Anonymous form** – the link listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md),
   if available.

Reports are treated confidentially. The reporter's identity is not shared with
the respondent or the public without explicit consent, except where required by
law. See [COC_REPORTING.md](COC_REPORTING.md) for the full investigation and
outcome process.

**Immediate risk:** If you or someone else faces an immediate physical threat,
contact local emergency services first. Notify the CoC Committee as soon as it
is safe to do so.

## Response and consequences

The CoC Committee determines proportionate responses following the process in
[COC_REPORTING.md](COC_REPORTING.md). Depending on severity, responses may
include:

- A private written warning.
- Temporary suspension from one or more project spaces.
- Permanent removal from all project spaces.
- Notification of the respondent's employer or platform provider where
  appropriate and legally permissible.

Threats of violence or doxxing are treated as severe violations and may result
in immediate, permanent removal without prior warning.

## Support resources

Contributors who have experienced harassment or a safety incident are
encouraged to seek support. The following resources are available globally:

- **Crisis Text Line** – text HOME to 741741 (US, UK, Canada, Ireland).
- **International Association for Suicide Prevention** –
  https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/ lists crisis centres by
  country.
- **Victim Connect Resource Center** – 1-855-4-VICTIM (US).
- **local emergency services** – dial your country's emergency number for
  immediate danger.

Maintainers can also connect contributors with project-specific support
contacts listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Scope

This policy applies to all spaces managed by the StellarEarn project,
including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, official chat
channels, mailing lists, video calls, and any in-person events held under the
project's name.

It also applies to behaviour outside these spaces when that behaviour
reasonably affects the safety or participation of community members inside them.

## Policy maintenance

This policy is reviewed at least once per year by the CoC Committee and
updated as needed. Substantive changes follow the standard governance
pull-request process and are announced in the project communication channel.
