# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

Contributor expenses have had no reimbursement policy. This defines what
expenses are eligible and how approval works.

## Eligible expenses

- Infrastructure costs directly required for approved project work (e.g. a
  testnet RPC endpoint, a CI runner, a domain renewal for a project-owned
  service).
- Travel or accommodation for representing the project at a conference or
  event, when approved in advance.
- Tooling or software licenses required for an approved contribution, when
  no free alternative meets the requirement.

Personal equipment, general living costs, and anything not directly tied to
an approved project task are not eligible.

## Approval process

1. Before incurring the expense, the contributor opens a request (issue or
   the project's designated request channel) describing the expense, amount,
   and the project work it supports.
2. A maintainer with treasury or budget authority approves or declines the
   request before the expense is incurred. Retroactive approval is only
   granted for genuine emergencies, and is the exception, not the norm.
3. Once approved, the contributor keeps a receipt or invoice and submits it
   with the original request for reimbursement.

## Payment and limits

- Reimbursement is paid via the project's standard payout method once the
  receipt is verified against the approved request.
- Requests above a materially large threshold (as set by the project's
  treasury policy) require sign-off from more than one maintainer.
- Disputed or unclear expenses are resolved by the maintainers with treasury
  authority; their decision is documented on the original request.
