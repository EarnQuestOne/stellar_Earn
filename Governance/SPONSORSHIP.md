# Sponsorship Acceptance Policy

This document defines the criteria and process for accepting sponsorships for the StellarEarn project. Sponsorships are distinct from grants: sponsors provide funding or in-kind support in exchange for defined visibility, while grants are awarded to advance project goals without commercial reciprocity.

## Acceptance Criteria

A sponsorship is accepted only when all of the following are met:

- **Mission alignment**: The sponsor's values and business do not conflict with StellarEarn's mission, Code of Conduct, or the interests of the Stellar ecosystem.
- **No editorial control**: The sponsor does not receive influence over technical roadmap, governance decisions, or personnel matters.
- **Transparency**: The sponsorship terms (amount or value, duration, deliverables) are recorded in a public agreement or disclosed in the project's financial transparency reports.
- **Brand usage**: Sponsor logo and name may appear only in agreed-upon locations (e.g., README sponsors section, event materials, website footer). No implied endorsement of the sponsor's products or services.
- **Exclusivity**: No category exclusivity is granted unless explicitly approved by the maintainers with treasury authority and documented in the agreement.
- **Termination**: Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. The project may terminate immediately for sponsor conduct violating the Code of Conduct or applicable law.

## Disclosure

All accepted sponsorships are:

- Listed in the project's financial transparency reports (see `docs/governance/FINANCIAL_TRANSPARENCY.md`).
- Recorded in a sponsorship register accessible to maintainers with treasury authority.
- Disclosed in any public communication where the sponsor receives visibility.

## Process

1. A sponsorship proposal is submitted via the project's designated intake channel (issue, email, or form).
2. Maintainers with treasury authority evaluate the proposal against the acceptance criteria.
3. If approved, a sponsorship agreement is signed (or terms confirmed in writing) documenting the items in **Acceptance Criteria**.
4. The sponsorship is added to the register and disclosed per **Disclosure**.
5. Deliverables are fulfilled per the agreement; maintainers verify compliance before each payout or renewal.

## Scope

This policy applies to all sponsorships: financial contributions, infrastructure credits, service donations, and event sponsorships. Grants are governed by `GRANTS.md`.
